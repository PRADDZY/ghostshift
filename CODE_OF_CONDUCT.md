# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Treat contributors and reviewers with respect. Be constructive, assume good intent, and keep discussion focused on the work.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks
- Doxxing or sharing private information without permission
- Spam, trolling, or intentionally disruptive behavior

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, issues, or pull requests that violate this code of conduct and may restrict future participation when needed to keep the project safe and productive.
